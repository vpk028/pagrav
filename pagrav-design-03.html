Pagrav rejects the cycle of novelty. We work in slow time. Every work begins in daily practice and ends in live performance, shared with an audience in the same room.

Pagrav rejects the cycle of novelty. We work in slow time. Every work begins in daily practice and ends in live performance, shared with an audience in the same room. Our company is built on the belief that Kathak is most alive when it is rigorous, contemporary, and shared in person.

We are not just making performances — we are shaping a contemporary vocabulary for Kathak, on stage and in the studio.
